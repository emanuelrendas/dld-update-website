Privacy

This website stores nothing. There is no database, no tracking pixel, no cookie, no mailing list. The brief form opens WhatsApp on your own device with a message you can read and edit before sending. Nothing reaches me until you press send.

Confidentiality

Client identities, transaction details and portfolio positions are never disclosed — not to other clients, not in marketing, not in any form. Any reference published here carries that client's written permission.

Sourcing Standard

Every figure on this site carries its source, publication date and confidence level — hover or tap any underlined number. Verified means an official release. Indicative means a range from registered transactions. Forecast means an assumption that may not occur. Where the intelligence map shows a Modelled tag, that figure is my own analysis rather than a published statistic, and the methodology is stated beside it. If a figure cannot be traced to an authoritative source, it is not published here.
